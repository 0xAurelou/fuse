# Production release gates

Status: **not approved for real-money launch**. The local integration milestone
and historical replays do not constitute prospective economic or public-chain
settlement evidence. No gate below is satisfied merely by merging this file.

The requested next engineering milestone is implemented in
`engine/internal/engine/recovery.go`, `engine/cmd/fuse-capture`, and
`scripts/analyze_capture.py`. Usage and limits are in
[backend risk and prospective capture](RISK_AND_CAPTURE.md). This is partial
progress on gates 1 and 3, not closure of either gate. All gates remain open until
an accountable reviewer signs the exact evidence for the release.

For each gate retain: release commit and artifact hashes, policy/configuration
hashes, dataset provenance and collection times, commands and environment,
complete raw results (including failures), reviewer identity/date, and decision.
Store immutable evidence under an access-controlled, backed-up release archive;
`.local` is a convenient working directory, not the production retention system.
A pass must identify an evidence URI and reviewer; missing or expired evidence
means the gate remains open. Economic thresholds not specified below require
explicit approval before observations are collected; do not invent thresholds
from observed results. Track remediation and revalidation when a dependency or
policy changes.

or a profitable replay never authorize a launch by themselves. Do not substitute
another replay of old recordings for new prospective data.
