# Degen market environments

Status: retained as an unused previous direction. The market cards now display the original Rose, Mint, and Violet portraits instead.

Created with the built-in Image Gen tool on 2026-10-26. Original PNGs are retained in the generation output directory. The project assets are 1200 × 500 WebP encodings, quality 84, with no creative alterations after generation.

The scenes are decorative fictional market identities. They contain no charts or market data. Text, feed state, price, and controls are rendered separately in the interface. The banners are limited to Degen mode. Hover zoom is disabled for reduced-motion preferences.
