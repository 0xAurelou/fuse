# After-hours chart artwork

The former six-tile Floor trombinoscope has been removed. Its original adult character images are retained, with [asset paths and generation prompts](club-cast-prompts.md), as artwork only. They are not rigged Spine or Live2D models. The [new character rigging brief](../../../docs/CHARACTER_ANIMATION_BRIEF.md) records the assets and runtime prerequisites for a genuine skeletal animation replacement.

The current Degen backdrop uses **rave-sigil.svg**, an original geometric tribal-style light pattern, with CSS laser fans and ambient neon gradients in `app/rave-lights.css`. Patterns rotate in opposite directions across the three charts, and the music panel carries a matching projection. The DJ toggle pauses every light layer; reduced-motion preferences disable the animations. Chart lines, prices and controls remain separate from the decorative layers. These effects use CSS timing and are not synchronized to the YouTube audio.

**nightclub.png** (1942 × 809) and the quieter **after-hours.png** are retained as previous background directions. The current **dj-cat.png** mascot (1254 × 1254, transparent) and both former backgrounds were generated using the built-in Image Gen tool. Their prompts are preserved below.

Absolutely no typography, logos, user interface, numbers or charts. Hand-drawn high-end 1990s anime cel art and printed manga zine texture, crisp ink outlines, very energetic and colorful, not 3D or realistic. Midnight-blue underground dance club lit with hot pink, electric lime, vivid violet and cyan; a gigantic glittering disco ball at upper center, laser light beams, little stars and luminous confetti. THREE stylish adult anime women clearly in their twenties, fully clothed, distinct original designs: left a woman with long pink hair and cat-ear headphones wearing an oversized cropped streetwear jacket and high-waisted trousers, dancing with an amused expression; center a cheerful mint-haired adult DJ behind turntables in a technical jacket; right a short lavender-haired adult woman with cat-ear headphones and a black oversized hoodie dancing. Include several cute charcoal and pink cartoon cats vibing and one playful cat curled on the DJ record. Big expressive faces, fun eccentric nightlife fashion, kawaii chaos, dopamine-rich character art. Composition: characters and cats grouped in lower half and along both sides so they remain visible when split into three equal vertical panels. Leave upper/middle area darker and lower-detail, suitable for overlaying white chart lines. Nightclub details throughout, not an empty city sky. Saturated but keep base dark navy to allow live chart readability. The brief is more anime girls, cats, spinning nightclub energy. No actual chart marks or trading data, no sexualized framing, no copied franchise characters. Opaque background.

## Previous rooftop direction

`after-hours.png` is original raster art generated with the built-in Image Gen tool, then copied into this project. It is used as a continuous panorama behind the three Degen charts. It contains no price data, UI or brand assets. The chart itself remains a live SVG data visualization. Original output: 1932 × 814 pixels.

Final generation prompt:

> Create a production web-app background illustration, wide landscape 1536x1024 or wider landscape composition, no UI, no chart lines, absolutely no typography or logos. Original hand-drawn anime/manga night-market arcade aesthetic, playful underground crypto-trading club but sophisticated editorial illustration, matte grainy paper, crisp inky outlines and restrained cel shading. Deep midnight navy and desaturated indigo base, pale lavender moonlight, electric lime and dusty pink accents. A stylish adult woman trader with short dusty-pink hair in an oversized black bomber jacket, confident relaxed expression, sits in the lower right at a rooftop arcade desk with a small mischievous charcoal cat. Distant dense city rooftops, cables, small luminous windows, paper tickets fluttering, tall crescent moon. Keep character cropped composition at far lower right 30% and some arcade silhouettes lower left; central 60% and upper area should be spacious dark-blue textured sky with sparse stars, not busy, because thin bright live trading plots and small labels will later overlay this asset. Think an after-hours illustrated zine cover, not glossy game concept art, no 3D, no photorealism, no neon gradients, no childish characters, no gambling text, no brand marks. Colorful but dark enough that white chart data reads clearly. Entire background opaque.
